App icon variants

Adaptive Android icon: navy background layer, drawing in the foreground layer. Everything important sits inside the safe circle in the middle, so it survives every shape a phone cuts the icon into. The last column is the single-colour layer used when "themed icons" are on. Sizes: 192 px (store / settings), 96 px and 48 px (home screen and notification shade).

1 · Quiet as a mouse

A mouse with a small speech bubble whose wave gets lower: "talk more quietly". In German: mucksmäuschenstill.

2 · The lion may not roar

The king of loud roars with its mouth wide open, but the roar is crossed out, like the elephant's trumpet.

3 · The elephant may not trumpet

Known for being loud and clumsy (the elephant in the china shop). Only the head, trumpeting, but the loud sound is crossed out: the well-known mute sign.
